Dear fellow,

Welcome to The Shadrach Fellowship. We are so glad you said yes. Over the coming year you will walk with a small cohort of leaders from across the world, learning to become a disciple who makes disciples: in your walk with God, in your family, in your work and in the nations.

Like Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, we want to be faithful wherever God has placed us, whatever the fire. That kind of faithfulness is not built in a day. It is built week by week: in the Word, in prayer, in honest community and in the people we pour our lives into. This pack tells you what to do first, what the year looks like and what we will ask of you.

We are praying for you, and we cannot wait to meet you at the ceremony.

With joy,
Frank Koomson
Coordinator, on behalf of the TSF team

The first weeks

Your weekly rhythm

  • Friday session, , online
  • Weekly reflection in the portal after each session: what you learned, your "I will", a Scripture, who you will share it with
  • Bible in One Year, a few chapters a day
  • Prayer calendar: the people God has given you, one day each
  • Your three disciples, met with regularly
  • Your Hi 5 prayer group, praying for an unreached people group

Books and tools

  • Disciple Ship: The Mega Mandate
  • Emotionally Healthy Spirituality by Peter Scazzero
  • Africa to the Rest (A2R)
  • The Bible in One Year app, free on your phone
  • The Fellows Portal for reflections, your Bible plan, prayer calendar, goals, disciples, assignments and quarterly reports

Across the year

  • A 21-day fast to open the year
  • A three-day intensive in July
  • Assignments and assessments, including DISC
  • A short self report at the end of each quarter
