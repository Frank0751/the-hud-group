My Commitment

I have counted the cost of this year and, by God's grace and with the support of my cohort, I commit to the journey. Tick each line as you read it, then sign.

  1. I will attend every Friday session, on time, and tell the coordinator before the session if I truly cannot.
  2. I will write my weekly reflection in the Fellows Portal after each session.
  3. I will read through the Bible in One Year and mark my progress in the portal.
  4. I will join the 21-day fast that opens the year.
  5. I will choose three disciples by the end of January and meet with them regularly.
  6. I will gather a Hi 5 prayer group and pray with them for an unreached people group.
  7. I will keep my prayer calendar and pray for the people God has placed on it.
  8. I will complete my assignments, assessments and quarterly reports on time.
  9. I will keep in confidence what my fellow fellows share in trust.
  10. I will stay teachable: honest with my facilitators and my cohort, and open to correction and encouragement.

"Let us not become weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap a harvest if we do not give up." Galatians 6:9
