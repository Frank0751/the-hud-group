The Journey

What you will gain as a Shadrach Fellow

Ten outcomes the fellowship is designed to grow in you over one year of weekly sessions, a mid-year retreat and a community that walks with you.

Fellows map their life stories and learn to see God at work in every season.

  • Deepen your relationship with the Triune God (intimacy)
  • Develop your emotional health. It is impossible to be spiritually mature while remaining emotionally immature.
  • Build Christ-like character as a follower of Jesus (imitation)
  • Discover and deploy your spiritual and natural gifts for every relevant sector (influence)
  • Learn practical life skills, from marriage to money, from a veteran mentor
  • Be built up, like Shadrach, as a vibrant marketplace influencer for the Kingdom of God (leader)
  • Catch a vision and develop a heart for God's world, especially Unreached People Groups
  • Acquire the mindset and skillset to make and multiply movements (disciple-making and church-planting)
  • Compose and adhere to Spirit-inspired personal vision and mission statements, values, goals and strategies
  • Join an intimate community of passionate friends, followers and fishers of Jesus Christ

The Format

One year, every Friday, wherever you are

The fellowship is hybrid. Fellows across Africa and beyond meet online every week, then gather in person once a year.

  • Weekly sessions onlineEvery Friday from 6 to 8 PM UTC on Zoom, so working professionals and students can take part.
  • The P3 Mid-Year RetreatOne immersive, in-person intensive in Ghana, typically in July.
  • Graduation in DecemberThe year closes with a graduation ceremony, and fellows go on to disciple others.
